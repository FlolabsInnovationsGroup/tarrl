In Memoriam
1931 – 2026

Admiral Bobby Ray Inman

Originator of the TARRL Vision · Chairman, TARRL and FloLabs Innovations Group · Admiral, U.S. Navy (Retired)

Admiral Bobby Ray Inman dedicated his life to public service, technological progress, education, and the development of future leaders. TARRL began with his idea: a vision to connect advanced technology and applied research with ambitious people solving meaningful problems.

His distinguished career included service as Director of Naval Intelligence, Director of the National Security Agency, and Deputy Director of Central Intelligence. Rising to the rank of four-star admiral, he helped shape an era of American national security leadership.

After his military service, he became an important voice in Austin's technology and entrepreneurship community. At The University of Texas at Austin, he shared his experience and encouragement with generations of students, researchers, and public servants.

The idea for the Texas Advanced Robotics Research Lab was Admiral Inman’s. As Chairman of TARRL and FloLabs Innovations Group, he helped shape a vision for bringing robotics, artificial intelligence, research, education, and entrepreneurship together to address real-world challenges. We are grateful for his original inspiration, leadership, time, and perspective.

We honor his memory by continuing to invest in capable people, rigorous inquiry, and opportunities for the next generation to make a meaningful contribution.

The Origins of TARRL

An idea that became a mission.

The inspiration for TARRL came from Admiral Inman. In conversations about preparing young people for a world transformed by robotics and artificial intelligence, he encouraged a different approach: give people an opportunity to explore, experiment, and discover through hands-on research.

During a July 10, 2025 discussion with John Hazlewood and Dean David (Tianwei) Ma of Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi, Admiral Inman argued for a genuine robotics laboratory rather than treating the initiative as a conventional classroom. Drawing on NASA's experimental work with robotic exploration, he emphasized curiosity, practical testing, and the value of giving students room to develop ideas of their own.

July 10, 2025 · A pivotal conversation His guidance helped clarify a foundational principle of TARRL: begin with a focused, hands-on laboratory, demonstrate working results, and build outward from what has been proven.

That discussion strengthened a vision connecting advanced robotics research, applied AI, university engagement, entrepreneurship, and experiential learning. Inman's leadership as Chairman of TARRL helped turn that inspiration into the lab's enduring purpose.
