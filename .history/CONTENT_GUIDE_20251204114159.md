## Instructions

1. Open https://strapi-production-9c3d.up.railway.app/admin
2. Click "Content Manager" in the left sidebar
3. Select either "Research Track" or "Benefit"
4. Click "Create new entry"
5. Fill in the fields from the data above
6. Click "Save" then "Publish"
7. Repeat for all entries

**Note:** The slug field will auto-generate from the name field.
